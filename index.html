About

A little about me.

I design AI products that people actually want to use.

I'm Om, a Product Designer at Supervity AI, working on AI-native products including agent platforms, enterprise workbenches, marketplaces and intelligent workflows. I focus on making complex AI systems feel simple, clear and trustworthy.

I've designed and shipped products for enterprise clients including Adani, NEC, ArcelorMittal, Oberoi and more, working across complex business workflows and real-world use cases.

I don't stop at the design file. I prototype, experiment with AI tools and build functional concepts to turn ideas into working experiences faster. And when I'm away from the screen, I'm usually sketching in pen and ink.

B.Des in UX Design from Amity University, with published research in design and HCI.
